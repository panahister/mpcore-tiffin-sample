# Demo media provenance

The menu pictures in `menu/` are synthetic demonstration assets generated on
2026-10-07 with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool. No third-party source
image, logo, restaurant identity or photographed person was supplied to the
generator. The restaurant names and menu are fictional.

Every prompt requested a square, photorealistic US restaurant catalog image,
the complete dish or glass in frame, natural restaurant lighting, no people,
no hands, no packaging, no text, no logo and no watermark. The subject and art
direction were:
